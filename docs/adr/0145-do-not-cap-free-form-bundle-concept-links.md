# Do Not Cap Free-Form Bundle Concept Links

GPT-5.5 free-form Bundled Purchase Lines have no fixed business-rule maximum for Purchase Line Concept Links. A source-backed combined commercial fact remains one bundle regardless of whether it contains two or many distinct Materials and Services, provided every link has exact Observed Name Text, valid Memory-Aware Name and taxonomy state, the bundle satisfies the shared-Provider boundary in ADR 0144, and every Installation Relationship is grounded. The existing Manual Source Entry and model request/output limits remain technical safeguards.

Habi never truncates excess links or silently drops an invalid link. Duplicate concept links, ungrounded links, or another invalid component make the bundle follow the configured repair and atomic-failure policy. Candidate Detail must support a scrollable or repeated linked-concept review layout rather than assuming a two-card maximum.
