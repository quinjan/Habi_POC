# Separately Priced Delivery Becomes a Service Line

When final/as-used source evidence presents delivery as a separately priced commercial fact tied to a Material purchase, Habi creates a source-ordered Delivery Service Purchase Line with its own price and Provider while retaining the Material's base price. This supersedes ADR 0140's annotation-only BuildMart example; included, unpriced, or merely operational delivery language remains Delivery Terms evidence and does not create a Service line.
